# Biology short-answer rubric

## Scientific accuracy (0-4 points)

- 4: The response is scientifically accurate and uses all relevant biological concepts.
- 3: The central explanation is accurate with one minor omission or imprecision.
- 2: The response shows partial understanding but contains a significant misconception.
- 1: The response contains a relevant fact but does not explain the process.
- 0: The response is incorrect, irrelevant, or blank.

## Evidence and reasoning (0-3 points)

- 3: The response cites specific evidence and clearly connects it to the conclusion.
- 2: The response includes relevant evidence, but the reasoning is incomplete.
- 1: The response makes an unsupported claim or lists evidence without connecting it.
- 0: No relevant evidence or reasoning is present.

Feedback should name one demonstrated strength and one specific next step. Do not deduct
for spelling or grammar unless it makes the scientific meaning unclear.
